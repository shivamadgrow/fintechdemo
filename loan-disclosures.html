Statutory Digital Lending Disclosures

Mandatory regulatory declarations and Key Fact Statement disclosures in full compliance with the Reserve Bank of India.

1. Role of Lending Service Provider (LSP)

Fintech Marketing Agency Private Limited acts purely as a Lending Service Provider (LSP) under the RBI Guidelines on Digital Lending (DOR.CRE.REC.66/21.07.001/2022-23).

  • We are NOT an NBFC or commercial bank and do not take credit risk on our balance sheet.
  • Loan disbursements flow directly from the regulated lender's bank account to the borrower's verified bank account without passing through any pool or pass-through account managed by the LSP.
  • All repayments are credited directly to the regulated lender through authorized e-NACH or BBPS channels.

2. Standardized Key Fact Statement (KFS) Model

Every borrower is provided a standardized Key Fact Statement in high-contrast legible format prior to entering any binding loan agreement. Below is an illustrative example:

Sanctioned Loan Principal ₹50,000
Annual Percentage Rate (APR) 14.5% p.a. (Reducing Balance)
Loan Tenure 12 Months (12 Installments)
Monthly Equated Installment (EMI) ₹4,501
Total Interest Payable Over Tenure ₹4,012
Upfront Processing Fee ₹1,000 + 18% GST (₹180) = ₹1,180
Total Amount Payable by Borrower ₹54,012
Net Disbursed Amount (Principal - Fee) ₹48,820
